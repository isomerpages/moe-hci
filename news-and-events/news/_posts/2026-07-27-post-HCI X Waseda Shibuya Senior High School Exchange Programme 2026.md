<p>On 20 and 21 Jul 2026, four of our students welcomed four buddies from
Waseda Shibuya Senior High School as part of the Ministry of Education
Language Centre’s (MOELC) local exchange programme.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/News and Events/WAS/waseda_shibuya_cover_page.jpg">
</div>
<p>Over the two days, the buddies attended lessons together, giving the Japanese
students a vibrant glimpse into life at Hwa Chong. Through a mix of Japanese
and English, they exchanged stories about their schools and everyday lives,
discovering both similarities and unique differences between the two cultures.</p>
