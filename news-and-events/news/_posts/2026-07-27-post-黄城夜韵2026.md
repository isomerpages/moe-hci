<p>5月15日、16日两天，华侨中学高中部大型戏剧演出 “黄城夜韵2026” 于佳伟剧院隆重上演。今年是黄城夜韵的45周年，演出以 “脉” 为主题，从人性挣扎、文化传承到自我追寻，呈现出一条贯穿代际的情感脉络，也映照出每一位黄城人台前幕后共同跳动的
“心脉”。当演出圆满落幕，这一届黄城夜韵所留下的，不只是舞台上的故事，更是一段关于坚持、协作与成长的集体记忆。</p>
<p>由黄城学生设计和亲手绘制的“印象黄城”艺术角也于5月7日开幕，为校园新添一道靓丽的风景。</p>
<p>（照片由“黄城夜韵”摄影组提供）</p>
<p><strong>An Evening of Drama 2026</strong>
</p>
<p>An Evening of Drama 2026, the 45th iteration of Hwa Chong Institution’s
annual student-led drama production, was held at Gateway Theatre on 15
and 16 May. This year’s production was themed <em>Pulse, </em>exploring
the subtle rhythms that connect humanity, culture and time. The three plays
traced stories of struggle, heritage and self-discovery, reflecting the
shared heartbeat that runs through generations of Huangcheng participants
— a testament to the passion, collaboration and the enduring spirit of
Huangcheng, where each performance becomes a lasting legacy.&nbsp;</p>
