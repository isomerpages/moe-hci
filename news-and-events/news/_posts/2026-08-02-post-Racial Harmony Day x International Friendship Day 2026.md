<p>Each year on 21 Jul, students from Hwa Chong Institution celebrate Racial
Harmony Day × International Friendship Day 2026, reaffirming our commitment
to a harmonious, multicultural, and beautiful society.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Racial/R1.png">
</div>
<p>Inspired by this year's theme, “Our People, Our Tapestry,” cultures met
through play and conversation, and students were reminded that harmony
is woven by understanding and respecting our differences.</p>
<p>
<br>
</p>
<p>Over at College, students enjoyed traditional games prepared by the Students'
National Education Council (SNEC), including Congkak, La Pirinola, Gasing,
Chapteh, Graces, Five Stones, and Cornhole. Others crafted friendship bracelets,
tying knots that represented simple yet meaningful symbols of connection.
In addition, students explored the traditional art of henna, discovering
the craftsmanship behind this centuries-old practice. Dressed in traditional
attire from different cultures, Hwa Chong students brought traditions to
life across the campus.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, over at the High School section, students commemorated Racial
Harmony Day on 23 Jul with a vibrant and engaging programme. The morning
kicked off at Kah Kee Hall with an assembly featuring a thought-provoking
talk by Defence Collective Singapore titled <em>“Is Race still an Issue in Singapore?”</em>.
Students then had the chance to visit student-led Heritage Traditional
Games Booths set up near the canteen. Co-organised by the National Education
Council and Consortium Councillors, these stations invited students to
try nostalgic Singaporean childhood games, with sweet treats like cup corn,
ice cream, kacang putih, and retro biscuits awarded as rewards!&nbsp;</p>
